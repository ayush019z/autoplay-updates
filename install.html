ANDROID INSTALL GUIDE

Install Cricket Auto Assist

Follow these steps on your Android phone or tablet to install and set up Cricket Auto Assist.

Download APK ↗Need help?

1Download the APK

Tap Download APK above to download the installation file.

2Pause Play Protect scanning

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, select Play Protect, and tap the settings gear. Turn off Scan apps with Play Protect temporarily to continue installation.

Important: Immediately after installing the APK, return to Play Protect settings and turn Scan apps with Play Protect back on. Keep Play Protect enabled for ongoing protection.

3Install and activate your license

Install Cricket Auto Assist, then activate your license key.

4Open Accessibility Settings

In Cricket Auto Assist, tap Open Accessibility Settings.

5Enable the accessibility service

In Android Settings, open Installed apps or Downloaded apps, select Cricket Auto Assist, and turn on Allow accessibility service. Menu names may vary by device.

6Review the accessibility disclosure

Read the accessibility disclosure carefully and confirm only if you agree to let the app assist with supported Discord game interactions.

7Open Discord and start

Return to Cricket Auto Assist, open Discord, and follow the in-app instructions to get started.

Common issues

  • Can't find the APK? Check the browser's Downloads list or your device's Downloads folder.
  • App not installed? Check that the download completed and that your device has enough free storage. Avoid uninstalling an existing version unless you understand the impact on its data.
  • Update or signature error? Don't uninstall the current app or install a different APK from an unofficial source. Contact support with the exact error message.
