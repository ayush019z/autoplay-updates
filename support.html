AutoPlay Support

Help with installation, updates, and license activation.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm you downloaded AutoPlay from the official website.
  • For updates, check that the latest release is published and that your internet connection works.
  • Never send passwords, account tokens, or your full license key in a public message.

Contact

Open the AutoPlay support/issues page to report a problem. Share your app version and a description of the issue, but do not include secrets or personal account details.
