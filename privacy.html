Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how AutoPlay (the Android app and related browser extension, where applicable) handles information. Please review it before using the software.

Information processed

  • License and activation: when you activate a license, the app or extension may send the license key, an installation/device identifier, and an activation or session token to the AutoPlay license service to validate access and check subscription status.
  • Preferences: settings and preferences are stored locally on your device or browser where supported.
  • Game interface: automation features may read visible interface text or controls needed to provide the feature you enable. Do not use the app with information you do not have permission to access.
  • Technical data: the hosting provider for the license service may process standard request data such as timestamps, IP addresses, and diagnostic logs.

How information is used

Information is used to validate licenses, provide the requested features, prevent unauthorized use, and troubleshoot service reliability. AutoPlay does not need your Discord password to activate a license. Never share passwords or account tokens with us.

Sharing and retention

License and technical information may be processed by the hosting providers needed to run the service. It is not intentionally sold for advertising. Data may be retained as needed for license operation, security, legal compliance, and troubleshooting; retention periods depend on the service logs and records involved.

Your choices and security

You can stop using the app and uninstall it. You can remove locally stored settings by clearing app/browser data. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security, so keep your license key private and use only official downloads.

Children

AutoPlay is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information was submitted to the service unnecessarily, contact support.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the app and service change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support page.
